Frequently asked questions

Grocers' questions

How does the credit book work?

When confirming the sale, choose “On credit” then the customer (or create them in a second). The receipt shows the total owed. When the customer repays, you record the amount and a receipt is printed.

Can I scan barcodes?

Yes, with the phone's camera (Chrome on Android) or with a USB or Bluetooth barcode reader, which works on all devices.

How do I track my stock?

Turn on “Track stock” on your items. Stock goes down with every sale; add goods received with the purchase price and the supplier, and correct after an inventory count.

What about products sold by weight or in packs?

Use options: for example “500 g / 1 kg / 2 kg” for tomatoes, or “Bottle / Pack of 6” for water, each with its own price.

How much does it cost?

30-day free trial, then a subscription depending on your type of business: at your country's price (see the Pricing page), 1 station and 5 sellers included, 2 months free when paying annually. Details on the Pricing page.
