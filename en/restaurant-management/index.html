Frequently asked questions

Restaurateurs' questions

How does the kitchen ticket work?

Open a table, add the dishes then tap “Send to the kitchen”. Only the new dishes are sent, without prices, with the table name. You can add dishes later and confirm the sale at the end of the meal.

Can I manage sides and extras?

Yes. Each dish can have groups of options: a required choice (side, portion) and optional extras, each with its own price.

What about takeaway?

Confirm the sale directly from the sales screen, or create a “Takeaway” table to send the order to the kitchen.

Do I need a printer?

No, it is optional: the receipt preview is shown on screen. To print, a 58 or 80 mm Bluetooth thermal printer is enough.

Can I send the bill via WhatsApp?

Yes: for a delivery or a phone order, tick “Remote customer” and the receipt opens in WhatsApp, ready to send.

How much does it cost?

30-day free trial, then a subscription depending on your type of business: at your country's price (see the Pricing page), 1 station and 5 sellers included, 2 months free when paying annually. Details on the Pricing page.
