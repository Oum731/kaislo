Stations and sellers

Beyond 5 sellers, one more station.

The manager

  • Creates their stations: “Counter”, “Terrace”, “Counter 2”…
  • Links each station to its printer
  • Creates their sellers and assigns them to a station
  • Sells from any station, without counting towards the limit

The station

  • The phone or tablet connected to the printer
  • up to 5 sellers
  • At the same time or in rotation
  • All their orders print on its printer

The seller

  • Assigned to one station at a time
  • The manager can move them to another station whenever they like
  • Works on their own phone, with their own login

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about pricing.

What is a station?

C’est un point d’impression : le téléphone ou la tablette relié(e) à l’imprimante. Il accueille 5 vendeurs au maximum, qui travaillent sur leur propre téléphone ; leurs tickets s’impriment tous sur l’imprimante du poste. Au-delà de 5 vendeurs, il faut un poste de plus.

Does the manager count as a seller?

No. The manager can sell from any station and never counts towards the limit.

Do I need a printer?

No, printing is optional: the receipt can go via WhatsApp or stay in the history. The printer is sold or rented separately if you want one.

How does the referral code work?

Did a Kaislo sales agent introduce you to the app and give you their code? Enter it when you create your account: you pay the price in the “With a referral code” column, about 10% less than the list price. The code is entered once, at sign-up.

How do I pay?

By mobile money (Wave, Orange Money, MTN MoMo…), bank transfer, or cash with the team. Monthly, or annually with 2 months free.

Are prices the same everywhere?

No: prices are shown in your country's currency, with a scale adapted to your market. If the detected country is not yours, change it above the table.
