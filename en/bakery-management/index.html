Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell very fast at peak hours?

Yes: each product is a big tile, one tap adds it to the receipt and confirming takes two seconds.

Do I need a printer?

No. The receipt can be sent via WhatsApp or simply kept in the history. A Bluetooth or USB thermal printer remains an option.

And without internet?

Kaislo keeps working. Sales are sent to the server as soon as the connection is back.

How much does it cost?

30-day free trial, then a subscription depending on your type of business: at your country's price (see the Pricing page), 1 station and 5 sellers included, 2 months free when paying annually. Details on the Pricing page.
