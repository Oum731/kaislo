How to find shops and products near you

Kaislo ranks the shops in your neighbourhood from the closest to the farthest. Allow your location, or choose your city, and you immediately see what is sold around you: grocery stores, bakeries, restaurants, clothing, phone and cosmetics shops and many more.

Each shop is run by an independent merchant. You browse its products and prices, then order directly from it, for pickup in the shop or for delivery when it offers it. The merchant confirms availability and you follow the status of your order.

Are you a merchant? Kaislo is also a cash register and stock management tool that works without internet, on a simple phone. See the presentation for sellers, or become a courier to earn money near you.

Shops are run by independent merchants. Kaislo is a tool that connects shops and their customers: it is not the seller and does not collect any payment. Confirm the price and availability with the shop.
