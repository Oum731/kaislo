Certified cash register (Horeca) in Belgium: what it is and what Kaislo does not replace

Belgian restaurants, cafes and caterers cannot choose just any POS software: the Horeca sector is subject to a certified cash register system. Here is the general idea, what Kaislo does and does not replace, and where to get the exact rules.

In this guide

  2. Who is concerned
  3. What Kaislo does not replace
  4. What Kaislo can complement

The general idea

To limit fraud, the Belgian administration requires the Horeca sector to use a certified cash register system: sales are recorded by a fiscal control module (often called the “black box”) linked to the till, which produces the official receipts.

The exact rules (who is concerned, which hardware, which dates) change: check them with the FPS Finance or your accountant, not in a software guide.

Who is concerned

Generally restaurants, cafes, bars, caterers and food-service activities. Retail businesses such as a grocery store or shop are not concerned in the same way, but obligations depend on your activity: ask your accountant.

What Kaislo does not replace

Kaislo is not a certified cash register system and does not issue the Horeca fiscal receipts. If your business is subject to it, keep your certified till to take payments and issue the official receipts.

  • Dish costing and margin.
  • Tracking losses and discrepancies.
  • Customer credit and the day’s accounts for your own management.

Frequently asked questions

Certified cash register (Horeca) in Belgium: what it is and what Kaislo does not replace

Is a certified cash register mandatory in Belgian Horeca?

Generally yes: the Horeca sector is subject to a certified cash register system. Check your situation with the FPS Finance or your accountant.

Does Kaislo replace my certified register?

No. Kaislo manages sales, stock and customer credit, but it is not a certified cash register system.

Can Kaislo still help in my restaurant?

Yes for stock, dish costs, losses and follow-up, alongside your certified register.
