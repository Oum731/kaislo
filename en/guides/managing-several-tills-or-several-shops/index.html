Managing several tills or several shops without losing control

As the business grows, you need several tills or a second shop. The challenge: keeping a single stock, clear accounts and simple control.

In this guide
  1. One stock, several tills
  2. One account per station and per seller
  3. Two shops: manage separately, review together
  4. Control remotely
  5. With Kaislo

One stock, several tills

When two sellers sell at the same time, every sale must lower the same stock. Use a tool where all devices sync, to avoid selling what no longer exists.

One account per station and per seller

  • Each till (station) has its own day opened and closed.
  • Each seller has their own access and permissions.
  • Gaps are then spotted station by station.

Two shops: manage separately, review together

For two points of sale, keep two separate businesses with their own stock and accounts, and compare them regularly: sales, margins, expenses. A lagging shop shows up immediately.

Control remotely

The manager should be able to see the day’s sales from their phone without visiting the shop. Check the dashboard in the evening and receive each station’s accounts.
