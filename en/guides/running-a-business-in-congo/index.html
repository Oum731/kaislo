Running a business in Congo: payments, currency and sales tracking

Whatever your business (shops and grocery stores, restaurants and bars, phone and electronics shops and depots and wholesalers), in Brazzaville or elsewhere in Congo, the same questions come up: how to set prices in FCFA, how to track payments by mobile money and in cash, and how to know every evening what the business really earned.

Currency and prices in Congo

In Congo, you sell in FCFA. The CFA franc has no cents: set prices in round amounts (for example multiples of 25 or 50 FCFA) so giving change is easy.

Update your selling prices every time your supplier’s price changes: an increase you do not pass on eats directly into your margin.

Common payment methods in Congo

Your customers can pay you by cash, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money and card. You do not have to offer all of them: keep the ones your customers really use and that you know how to check.

With MTN MoMo and Airtel Money, the money lands on your phone or account, not in the till: that is why you must record the payment method on every sale.

Record each method and reconcile every evening

  • Record every sale with its payment method.
  • In the evening, take each method’s total from your accounts.
  • Compare each mobile money total with the credits in your app.
  • Count the cash and compare it with expected cash: the difference is the cash gap.
  • Record fees (withdrawal, transfer, commission) as an expense.

The most common businesses in Congo

In Congo, you mostly find shops and grocery stores, restaurants and bars, phone and electronics shops and depots and wholesalers. In all of them, three habits make the difference: track stock, know your margin and never mix business money with personal money.

Legal, tax and licensing rules vary by country and activity: check with the authorities or a local accountant before you start.
