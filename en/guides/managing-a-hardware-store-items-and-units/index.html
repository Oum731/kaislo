Hardware store: managing thousands of items, units and prices

Screws, cables, paint, cement, pipes: a hardware store sells hundreds of items in different units. Organisation makes the difference between finding things fast and losing sales.

In this guide
  1. One item, one unit
  2. Find an item quickly
  3. Quantity prices and professional customers
  4. Stock: watch rotation
  5. With Kaislo

One item, one unit

Each item needs a clear selling unit: piece, metre, kilo, carton, bag, roll. Electric cable sells by the metre, nails by the kilo, cement by the bag. Also note the purchase unit if it differs (for example a carton of 100).

Find an item quickly

  • Use a clear, consistent name (type, size, brand).
  • Sort by family: plumbing, electrical, paint, tools.
  • Use product barcodes or your own labels.
  • Store by family and label the locations.

Quantity prices and professional customers

Craftsmen and building sites buy in quantity and expect a better price. Decide your tiers and discounts in advance, and keep credit for regular customers, with a ceiling and a deadline.

Stock: watch rotation

A few items make up most of your sales (cement, pipes, common cables). Always keep them in stock with an alert level, and limit slow-moving items that tie up your money.

Frequently asked questions

Hardware store: managing thousands of items, units and prices

How do you sell the same item in several units?

Create separate items for each selling unit (per metre, per roll) or choose a main unit and record the quantity sold.

How do you track credit for craftsmen?

Record each credit sale under the customer’s name with a ceiling and deadline, and follow the balance owed in a book.
