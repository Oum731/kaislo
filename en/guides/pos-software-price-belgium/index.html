POS software price in Belgium: how much to budget

The advertised price of POS software is only part of the budget. Between the subscription, hardware, the card terminal and possible commissions, the gap can be large. Here are the items to compare and Kaislo’s prices.

The five cost items

  • The software subscription or licence (monthly, yearly or one-off).
  • Hardware: till, tablet, cash drawer, printer, barcode scanner.
  • The card payment terminal, which depends on your bank and not on the software.
  • Possible commissions on payments or on sales.
  • Set-up, training and support, sometimes billed separately.

Compare like for like

A low price with no stock or customer credit does not replace a slightly higher price that includes them. Always compare the same list: till, stock, customer credit, data export, offline use, number of tills and sellers.

Ask what changes if your business grows: a second till, a second location, more sellers.

What Kaislo does not charge for

No mandatory hardware: Kaislo runs on the phone, tablet or computer you have. No commission on your sales: customers pay you directly, and Kaislo never collects their money. The receipt printer is optional.

Restaurants and cafes: plan for the certified register

In Belgium, restaurants, cafes and caterers (the Horeca sector) must use a certified cash register system linked to a fiscal control module (the “black box”). Kaislo is a tool for managing sales, stock and customer credit: it is not that certified system and does not replace it. Check your obligations with the FPS Finance or your accountant.
