Notebook, Excel or POS software: how to choose for your business

Many businesses start with a notebook, then an Excel file. It works while activity stays small. Here is how to know when to move to software, and what to look at before choosing.

In this guide
  1. The notebook: simple but fragile
  2. Excel: powerful but manual
  3. Software: the sale updates everything
  4. Questions to ask before you choose
  5. Where Kaislo fits

The notebook: simple but fragile

A notebook costs nothing and everyone can use it. But it gets lost or wet, calculates nothing and cannot tell you quickly what is left in stock or how much a customer owes. Addition mistakes go unnoticed.

Excel: powerful but manual

A spreadsheet can calculate, sort and keep a history. But everything has to be typed in afterwards, the file lives on one device, two people cannot sell at once, and one accidentally deleted formula throws off every figure.

Software: the sale updates everything

With point-of-sale and management software, each sale lowers stock, feeds the evening accounts and updates the credit book. Several sellers can work at once, and the figures are available at any time.

Questions to ask before you choose

  • Does it work without internet and send data when the network returns?
  • On which devices: phone, tablet, computer?
  • Does it handle stock, customer credit and the payment methods of my country?
  • Does each seller have their own access and limited rights?
  • Can I export my data (Excel, PDF) and take it with me if I stop?
  • What is the real monthly price, with no hidden fees?
