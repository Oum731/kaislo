## Long description

**EN**
Jump on your dirt bike and race 5 rivals across 3 spectacular tracks: a rolling forest, a desert canyon and an alpine glacier.

- **Three ways to play**: quick race, a 3-round **championship** with points and a trophy, and **time trial** where you race your own ghost.
- **Stunts**: front flips, back flips and whips. Land cleanly for nitro and combos — miss the landing and you crash!
- **Progress and rewards**: earn coins and XP, level up, complete objectives for ⭐ stars, unlock 16 achievements and a daily bonus.
- **Garage**: buy 4 different bikes, upgrade engine, suspension and nitro, and repaint them.
- **3 difficulty levels**, tracks to unlock by finishing on the podium.
- **Play anywhere**: desktop and mobile, right in your browser, no download. Available in English, French, Spanish, Portuguese and German.

## Controls / instructions
**EN** — Arrow keys or WASD: accelerate, brake, steer · Shift or Space: nitro · In the air: ↑ front flip, ↓ back flip, ← → whip · R: restart · M / N: sound / music. On mobile: on-screen buttons.
**FR** — Flèches ou WASD : accélérer, freiner, tourner · Shift ou Espace : nitro · En l'air : ↑ front flip, ↓ back flip, ← → whip · R : recommencer · M / N : son / musique. Sur mobile : boutons à l'écran.
**ES** — Flechas o WASD: acelerar, frenar, girar · Shift o Espacio: nitro · En el aire: ↑ front flip, ↓ back flip, ← → whip · R: reiniciar · M / N: sonido / música. En móvil: botones en pantalla.
**PT** — Setas ou WASD: acelerar, frear, virar · Shift ou Espaço: nitro · No ar: ↑ front flip, ↓ back flip, ← → whip · R: reiniciar · M / N: som / música. No celular: botões na tela.
**DE** — Pfeiltasten oder WASD: Gas, Bremse, Lenken · Shift oder Leertaste: Nitro · In der Luft: ↑ Frontflip, ↓ Backflip, ← → Whip · R: Neustart · M / N: Ton / Musik. Mobil: Bildschirmtasten.
