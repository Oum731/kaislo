=== APEX RIDER GP — FICHE DE SOUMISSION / SUBMISSION SHEET ===

TITLE
Apex Rider GP

CATEGORY (suggested)
Racing / Sports (Motorcycle)

TAGS
motorcycle, moto, racing, motogp, bike, 3d, simulation, career, rain, night, neon, tuning, garage, pit stop

SHORT DESCRIPTION (EN)
Race a superbike through a neon city at night. Tune your bike in the garage, manage tyre wear, survive the rain and climb from 300cc to the 1000cc Apex GP class.

DESCRIPTION (EN)
Apex Rider GP is a 3D motorcycle racing game with a full career loop. Upgrade your engine, exhaust, suspension and brakes, choose your tyres, pick a sponsor and hit the track. Tyres wear and heat up differently on the left and right sides, brakes fade, and the weather turns to heavy rain mid-race: the racing line dries first, so stay on it. Crash hard enough and your bike is out. Call a pit stop and win time back with the tyre-change mini-game. Spend your XP in the rider skill tree and unlock the 600cc and 1000cc classes.

DESCRIPTION COURTE (FR)
Pilotez une superbike dans une ville néon de nuit. Réglez votre moto au garage, gérez l'usure des pneus, survivez à la pluie et passez du 300cc au 1000cc Apex GP.

INSTRUCTIONS / CONTROLS
Keyboard: Left/Right (or A/D) lean - Up (or W) throttle - Down (or S) front brake - Space rear brake - Q/E shift weight forward/back - B pit stop - 1-4 choose next tyres - Esc pause.
Gamepad: left stick lean - RT throttle - LT front brake - A rear brake - right stick weight.
Mobile: on-screen buttons (arrows, throttle, brakes, pit stop, pause).

LANGUAGE
No text in the interface (icons and numbers only) - playable in any language.

CONTROLS TYPE
Keyboard, gamepad, touch.

ORIENTATION
Landscape.

RECOMMENDED SIZE
960 x 540 (responsive, supports fullscreen)

SDK
GameMonetize SDK integrated (pause/resume events + interstitial between races, never before the 2nd race and at most every 2 minutes).
Replace VOTRE_GAME_ID by your Game ID: GM_ID=<your id> npm run build
